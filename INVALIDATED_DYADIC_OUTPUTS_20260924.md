# 2026 dyadic fight output version gate

The first 2026 fight post-processing batch used persistent-group model 1.2.0.
It is withdrawn for biological interpretation. A focused audit of ten
high-PSC cases found five in which the older rule placed a beetle on a pile
despite a separate raw-labeled blob. The old finalizer Slurm array `898686`
was canceled. Original student TOMLs, IDtracker tracking sessions, trajectories,
immutable submission receipts, and tracking logs remain source evidence.

On 24 September 2026, the local model-1.2.0 dyadic smoke PDF/video/HTML/quality
copy for task `01d390290e38aced` was deleted, as were its superseded local
quality-summary CSV/JSON. The local tracking-status and TOML-geometry reports
were retained because they do not depend on PSC rules.

The replacement release gate is **PSC model 1.3.0 plus group processor
0.8.1-group** (code commit `d7e14ab`). Every released dyadic task must have a
manifest declaring both versions, `COMPLETE_UNREVIEWED` status, raw and PSC
window/region/fungus comparison CSVs, paired PDF tables, a frame-by-frame file
with separate raw and PSC metrics, a quality report, and a synchronized
HTML/video review. The two model-1.3.0 validation jobs originally submitted
under Slurm array `898766` used the earlier 0.8.0-group report schema; they
are superseded previews and must not be packaged as final paired reports.

One structurally eligible TOML found zero blobs with the requested background
subtraction and remains a documented tracking exclusion. Four additional
structurally incompatible TOMLs were explicitly excluded by Vince. The other
99 distinct trackable configurations require versioned reruns. None of these
reviews is ground truth until a person checks its identities and pile events
against the video.
