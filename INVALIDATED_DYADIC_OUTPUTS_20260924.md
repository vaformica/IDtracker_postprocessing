# 2026 dyadic fight output version gate

The first 2026 fight post-processing batch used persistent-group model 1.2.0.
It is withdrawn for biological interpretation. A focused audit of ten
high-PSC cases found five in which the older rule placed a beetle on a pile
despite a separate raw-labeled blob. The old finalizer Slurm array `898686`
was canceled. Original student TOMLs, IDtracker tracking sessions, trajectories,
immutable submission receipts, and tracking logs remain source evidence.

On 24 September 2026, the local model-1.2.0 dyadic smoke PDF/video/HTML/quality
copy for task `01d390290e38aced` was deleted, as were its superseded local
quality-summary CSV/JSON. The local tracking-status and TOML-geometry reports
were retained because they do not depend on PSC rules.

The historical, now superseded replacement gate was **PSC model 1.3.0 plus group processor
0.8.1-group** (code commit `d7e14ab`). Every released dyadic task must have a
manifest declaring both versions, `COMPLETE_UNREVIEWED` status, raw and PSC
window/region/fungus comparison CSVs, paired PDF tables, a frame-by-frame file
with separate raw and PSC metrics, a quality report, and a synchronized
HTML/video review. The two model-1.3.0 validation jobs originally submitted
under Slurm array `898766` used the earlier 0.8.0-group report schema; they
are superseded previews and must not be packaged as final paired reports.

One structurally eligible TOML found zero blobs with the requested background
subtraction and remains a documented tracking exclusion. Four additional
structurally incompatible TOMLs were explicitly excluded by Vince. The other
99 distinct trackable configurations require versioned reruns. None of these
reviews is ground truth until a person checks its identities and pile events
against the video.

## Further invalidation: shared PSC model 1.3.0

The six-beetle Box01_MIX review exposed an additional defect in the same
shared `persistent_groups.py` engine: its split-confirmation branch could
attach a hidden member to a raw identity hundreds of pixels from its previous
pile, bypassing the normal speed gate. Consequently, every dyadic
model-1.3.0/processor-0.8.1-group postprocessing output is also **WITHDRAWN**
for biological interpretation, including the two high-conflict validations.
Slurm array `899144` was canceled. IDtracker tracking sessions, source TOMLs,
logs, and immutable receipts were retained.

The next candidate is shared PSC model 1.4.0 at commit `0e61a75`, paired with
group processor 0.8.1-group. The same rule is used for two and six beetles;
it rejects impossible split jumps and brief distant raw-ID bursts. When
identity cannot be assigned at a multiway split it records unknown location,
not an invented contact path. Only new `v140r081` analyses can be considered
for a replacement release, and they remain unreviewed until compared with
video. The 99 trackable configurations and one documented no-blob exclusion
are the intended batch scope; four incompatible TOMLs remain excluded by
Vince.

## Further invalidation: shared PSC model 1.4.0

The shared model also failed to revise a long false pile assignment after
beetle 0 later reappeared on a smooth raw IDtracker track in six-beetle
`groupagetrial01_062024_Box02_MIX` (frames 14012–14565). Because dyadic
postprocessing calls the same shared code, all derived model-1.4.0 dyadic
outputs are withdrawn pending validation under a new model. Array 899414 was
canceled; original tracking sessions, TOMLs, logs, and exclusion receipts are
preserved. PSC model 1.5.0 was a candidate, not a released dataset. It
adds conservative retrospective tracing of unique unlabeled blobs and an
explicit raw/PSC identity-conflict flag for video review.

## Preliminary PSC 1.5.0 candidate withdrawn before release

The six-beetle Box02_MIX backtrace left nine unsupported pile frames before
the first separate unlabeled blob appeared. Because dyadic analysis uses the
same shared engine, preliminary dyadic array 899462 was canceled and its
1.5.0-derived outputs are withdrawn. Model 1.5.1 marks this short departure
interval as unknown location and excludes it from pile movement and fungus
tallies. Source TOMLs, IDtracker tracking, and prior exclusion receipts remain
untouched. Complete dyadic replacement requires new versioned outputs.

## PSC 1.3.0 derived-file removal and replacement version

On 24 September 2026, Vince requested deletion of withdrawn 1.3.0 results.
Within the mixed dyadic batch root, Codex removed 708 version-suffixed 1.3.0
derived items while retaining tracking sessions, source TOMLs, receipts, and
the current pipeline. Together with the two isolated GroupTrial roots, the
Firebird removal covered 710 targets, 3,950 files, and 119 generated review
videos. The exact paths and results are recorded in
`idtracker_pipeline_runs/PSC_1_3_0_REMOVAL_INVENTORY_20260924.json` and
`PSC_1_3_0_REMOVAL_RESULT_20260924.json`. A post-removal scan found no
model-1.3.0 analysis manifests remaining under `idtracker_pipeline_runs`.

The dyadic replacement runs use **PSC model 1.5.1** and **report processor
0.8.1-group** (code commit `934b587`). Two difficult validations completed;
array `899555` was submitted for the other 97 trackable configurations using
immutable `code_v151r081_release`. The four structurally incompatible TOMLs
and one no-blob tracking failure remain excluded. Every usable output must
record both versions in `analysis_v151r081/manifest.json` and remain marked
UNREVIEWED until a person checks its identities against video.
