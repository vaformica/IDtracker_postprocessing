Six beetles, three fungus ROIs

Version 0.2.1-six-beetle. Scientific primitives are imported from processor.py v0.8.1. Outputs require human review.

Candidate proximity, disappearance and turtling are screens for video review. They do not establish attacks, physical contact, dominance, or upside-down posture. No coordinates are interpolated or replaced.

Coordinate system and analysis windows

Let pi(f) = (x,y) be the selected IDtracker centroid for animal i (0 through 5) at global frame f. Arena is ROI 0; fungi are ROIs 1, 2 and 3 in input order. Vince specifies left-to-right numbering, with fungus 1 described as the leftmost or bottommost fungus bracket; polygons are not automatically reordered. All geometry uses the original coordinate system in pixels. Analysis runs from positive global start s through e = s + W inclusive: W intervals and W + 1 observations. No later coordinate replaces a missing exact-start baseline.

Wake

Wake is the first frame f for which ||pi(f) − pi(s)|| ≥ T, reachable from s through an uninterrupted chain of finite adjacent coordinates and steps ≤ J. Default T = 30 pixels and J = 200 pixels. Once a missing coordinate or rejected step breaks this chain, a later crossing cannot repair it. Latency is f − s. This is a displacement crossing, not sustained activity.

Three windows are reported: full [s,e], individual post-wake [wakei,e], and fixed [max(wake0…5), max(wake0…5)+3600]. The fixed window requires all six wakes and all 3601 observations within [s,e]. Unavailable rows have a status and blank measurements; region rows are emitted only for available windows.

Movement and denominators

For adjacent frames define d(f) = ||p(f+1) − p(f)||. A step is accepted only if both endpoints are finite and d(f) ≤ J. Total distance D = Σ accepted d(f). Rejected steps never remove endpoint coordinates from frame-location summaries. There is no bridging across gaps or jumps.

Distance per valid step = D / accepted-step count. Distance per available step = D / (window-end − window-start). Both are blank when there are no accepted steps; a measured stationary step contributes zero and remains accepted. A one-observation window has no movement estimate. Missing-endpoint and rejected-jump counts are mutually exclusive. Missing proportion = missing coordinate observations / all coordinate observations.

Spatial measurements

Centroid membership assigns frame counts; midpoint m(f) = (p(f)+p(f+1))/2 assigns accepted movement steps and their full distance. Polygon membership uses the baseline ray-crossing algorithm, including points within 10−9 pixels of the boundary. Distance to a polygon boundary is the minimum Euclidean distance to its finite line segments.

A wall frame or midpoint lies inside the arena and within the configured wall buffer of its boundary. Non-wall is its complement among finite coordinates, including outside-arena coordinates, which are also reported separately. Open arena means inside arena, outside wall buffer, and outside every fungus.

Each fungus edge buffer lies inside that fungus within the configured distance of its boundary. Interior is inside fungus but farther from its edge. Any-fungus is the union of all three polygons. Thus any-fungus + off-fungus = finite observations, and wall + non-wall = finite observations; the corresponding distance sums equal total accepted distance. Each fungus edge + interior equals that fungus. Individual fungi may overlap and are therefore not additive across fungus IDs. ROI polygons must be reviewed for correct shape, input order and coordinate alignment.

All 15 social pairs

For each unordered pair i < j, proximity(f) = both coordinates finite AND ||pi(f) − pj(f)|| ≤ R, with R = 60 pixels by default. Proportion uses jointly visible frames as denominator; no jointly visible frames means a blank proportion. Contiguous true runs are proximity bouts. A visible-return event requires proximity, an intervening run with at least one jointly visible frame farther than R, then proximity again. Missingness alone is not visible separation.

A disappearance candidate is a bounded run where one specific animal is missing, the other is visible throughout, both were within R immediately beforehand, and both are visible on return. The return need not itself be within R, matching the baseline rule. Missing runs reaching a window boundary are not completed candidates. All pairs are evaluated independently. The number of partners within R immediately before disappearance is preserved; more than one marks ambiguous attribution. Even a single candidate is not confirmed contact. No centroid substitution is performed.

Individual any-partner frames are the union of incident pair masks. Multiple-partner frames have at least two incident masks. Individual social distance sums accepted steps whose two endpoints each have at least one nearby partner; the partner may change between endpoints. It is not a pair-specific movement measure. Full-window and available fixed-window pair screens are recomputed within their respective boundaries. Pair durations cannot be summed to obtain elapsed group contact time.

Three-color frame-state timeline

Each animal and global frame has exactly one display state. Presumed social contact (gold) takes priority if the animal is within the proximity threshold of any visible partner, or is either participant in a bounded disappearance candidate. Otherwise a finite coordinate is observed/ID assigned (blue), and an absent coordinate is missing (orange). Hatching marks all participants in an ambiguous multi-partner disappearance candidate. This convention does not establish physical contact or attacks and does not modify coordinates, missing counts, or movement calculations.

The exported frame_states.csv independently records coordinate_present (0/1), proximity_partner_count (0–5), disappearance_candidate (0/1), and ambiguous_disappearance (0/1). Thus a gold frame may still have a missing coordinate. Observed refers to the selected source, which may include IDtracker gap filling. The full-window plot may visually compress brief events; CSV retains every inclusive frame without downsampling. The 15-pair timeline remains a separate page: teal marks observed proximity and gold marks the exact pair’s qualifying disappearance intervals, with hatching for ambiguous partners. It uses full-window pair events, not the intersection of individual contact states; two animals may be interacting with different partners. Blank intervals mean no qualifying evidence, not confirmed absence of contact. Exact inclusive intervals are retained in events.csv.

Turtling screen

The v0.8.1 tight-loop screen is reused with its defaults: 120-frame window, minimum path 120 pixels, maximum 90th-percentile radius 35 pixels, minimum accumulated turning 3 rotations, maximum straightness 0.25, maximum step 20 pixels. Candidate frames on any fungus are removed. Remaining contiguous runs are candidate events. Frame counts and event counts are reported per analysis window; red points in individual maps show full-window candidate frames. See the existing METHODS.html for the detailed baseline algorithm.

Version 0.3.0 report additions

Each beetle has a full-page trace. Orange X markers denote the last assigned coordinate immediately before each missing run; no position is inferred within gaps. The 3D plot uses x/y pixels and seconds from analysis start when a verified frame rate is supplied, otherwise global frame. Paths omit missing and rejected transitions. The fungus_summary table has one row per beetle, with full-window occupancy frames, occupancy seconds (frames divided by supplied FPS), and existing accepted-step midpoint distances for each fungus. PDF names match the TOML stem when supplied via --pdf-name, or default to video stem plus cell label. The report contains 12 pages.

Version 0.4.0: presumed social disappearance

This supersedes earlier no-substitution display statements. Missing IDs follow a unique visible anchor when proximity held in the preceding frame. Groups can grow transitively beyond two beetles. Returning IDs detach to their measured locations. Multiple possible anchors or no visible anchor leave positions unresolved; no future return is required. Inference runs from the beginning of the trajectory before slicing the analysis window. Raw coordinates and metrics remain separate from estimated coordinates and estimated metrics. Solid paths are raw; dashed paths are presumed social disappearance. Gold individual states denote group membership, including its visible anchor, and not proximity alone. The 13-page report includes both observed and estimated fungus tables.
