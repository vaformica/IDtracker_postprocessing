Quality, definitions, and downloads

Proximity bouts are contiguous intervals of at least 15 frames with centroid distance ≤60 original-image pixels. Threshold jitter may fragment bouts. Approach estimates use the preceding second; departure estimates use the following second. Missing boundaries are censored. These describe contributions to distance change, not intent or causation.

Motion requires a unique individually identified blob within 5 px of the raw centroid throughout the motion window, with no step above 20 px. No missing intervals are filled. Body axes require silhouette axis ratio ≥1.4. Shared silhouettes have no individual axis. Motion windows use centered estimates and cannot establish exact response latency.

Source coordinates may already be gap-processed by idtracker.ai. Independent-blob checks reduce this concern but do not certify identity. Overlay geometry follows the existing crop/rotation; measurements use original-image pixels.
