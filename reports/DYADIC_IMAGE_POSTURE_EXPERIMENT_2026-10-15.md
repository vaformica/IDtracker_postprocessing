# Dyadic image posture experiment — October 15, 2026

Vince requested an image-based test after his scores showed both fighting-related false candidates and missed quiet overturned beetles. This is a separate **0.1.0-image-development** experiment. Production PSC, BA, six-beetle processing and original research data are unchanged.

## What was tested

A fixed, regularized logistic model uses 76 image appearance and temporal-change features from 90-by-90-original-pixel target-centered crops, resampled to 96-by-96 to retain native detail for feature extraction. Three sampled images span one second, centered on the evaluated frame (15 frames before and after). Features describe local contrast, edges, texture and changes in independently centered crops. Coordinates place the raw-ID crop only; absolute position, identity, recording, frame number, proximity, fungus status and centroid-motion metrics are not classifier inputs. This is an appearance baseline, not a trained anatomical pose detector or a semantic classifier of fighting.

Only explicit VAF on-back/not-turtling intervals from both exports supply labels. Duplicate run/ID/frame labels are merged; there were no conflicting labels. Overall assessments without intervals remain unscored. The full-span negative interval in round-two clip 5 is retained with its unchecked viewing checkbox as a provenance caveat. Flailing is unassessed. Model scores are uncalibrated; 0.5 was fixed before evaluation. Each entire source recording, including its other cells, is excluded from its own training fold. This avoids same-recording training leakage, but the small, targeted samples do not estimate population accuracy.

The preliminary compressed-review-image attempt omitted crucial edge-of-arena examples and performed poorly. It is preserved in the parent directory as a diagnostic, not the delivered test. The final attempt uses native-resolution frames from original Firebird videos with 60-pixel additional arena margins. Lossless FFV1 sampled grayscale crops and higher-quality review videos were streamed locally through a read-only CPU allocation; no remote output folder or group-permission change was made. Original-video path/size/mtime and derived-file SHA-256 values are preserved in extraction receipts. Whole original recordings were not rehashed. Native samples were extracted every 15 frames, exactly by decoded-frame selection; no interpolation or inferred/PSC centers were supplied. Missing target positions and unusable crops are unassessed.

## Held-recording-out diagnostic

Across the available explicitly labeled sampled images: **{'TP': 110, 'FN': 66, 'TN': 50, 'FP': 98}**. These are sampled-image counts, not full-frame durations, independent animals or an unbiased accuracy estimate. The same-sample motion-rule states and per-example image results are in `COMPARISON.json`. Per-job counts overlap and must not be summed. Missing crop coverage is recorded separately in `VALIDATION.json`.

**This first image model did not reliably improve the detector.** It misclassified all 35 sampled positive images from the original quiet-beetle excerpt, while recognizing six of ten sampled positive images in the originally missed round-two clip 6. In the round-two fighting clip 4, it falsely labeled 37 of 38 sampled negative images on back. Across the deduplicated scored samples there were 66 missed positives and 98 false positives (164 disagreements / 324 samples). Do not compare that directly with a motion-rule error rate without preserving the motion rule’s interaction-ambiguous abstentions. The same-sample states are retained in COMPARISON.json. Fighting and on-back posture may coexist, so they must not become mutually exclusive biological labels. Keep all automatic outputs as review hypotheses until independent human scoring supports them.

## New small review

Six 15-second clips (90 seconds total) show native original-video arena crops plus a magnified, unlabeled target crop. Two are familiar diagnostic follow-ups: the missed on-back event and fighting. Four are unseen time windows chosen using the preliminary image/motion disagreements, uncertainty and a low-score control. Selection was frozen before the native-image fit, and was not changed to favor successful native predictions. The same source recording is excluded from model training for every displayed prediction. Familiar clips are not blind, and the other clips are targeted rather than random. Predictions stay hidden in the scoring UI.

Open `START_HERE.html` via the local reviewer. Mark explicit On back, Not turtling, Uncertain or Not visible intervals, then export **Dyadic_posture_image_scores_20261015.json**.
