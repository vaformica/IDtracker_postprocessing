A reviewer can skim each beetle’s path, play a short excerpt or the full video, and flag jumps, missing tracking, possible turtling, lighting problems, or the wrong tracked object. Decisions retain the reviewer, inspected scope, notes and marked times. An exclusion recommendation does not delete data.

Turtling is visible and can be flagged by hand

Automatic turtling candidates appear in purple on the path, timeline and video. Human-marked possible-turtling intervals appear in pink and are exported separately. The frozen 0.4 development rules were applied to these proposed strict tracks; this is a new screening application, not independent validation.
