What changed

Each event now shows the first local mover, entry contributor, and leaver separately. Entry is at one estimated body length or an existing PSC. Exit requires 0.20 seconds continuously beyond 1.2 body lengths and is timestamped at the first outside frame. The exit buffer is an exploratory anti-jitter choice, not a validated biological threshold. Brief candidates are retained; unknown intervals without PSC split observations with unknown exit.

Body length is the per-animal median 5th–95th percentile major-axis silhouette extent on the previously usable elongated individual detections. Pair thresholds use the mean of those two medians. These are provisional shape-based estimates, not anatomical calibration. Dyadic 2 uses approximately 45.2 pixels; Dyadic 1 approximately 40.0 pixels.

Motion attribution uses contiguous independent detections adjacent to the boundary, within two body lengths and at most one second. No PSC positions are used for velocity. Estimates must agree across available 0.20/0.40/0.80-second contexts (shorter contexts can be reused at larger requested windows; this is not three independent tests). First mover means the first visible sustained local onset, not the origin of a long-range approach. Onsets already underway at the context boundary remain unknown. Full rules and source hashes are in each PROVENANCE.json; events.json retains window contributions and context indices, relative to video start.

PSC is imported from frozen PSC 1.6.0 same-group, finite-location intervals with at least one inferred member. Its production threshold remains unchanged. It is separately marked, including intervals without measurable individual separation; six-beetle pair membership may be indirect through a group. These are inferred contacts, not proof of an observed one-body-length crossing.
