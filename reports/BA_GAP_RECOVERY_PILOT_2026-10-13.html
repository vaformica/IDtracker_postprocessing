Checks and uncertainty

38 synthetic gaps were created within human-accepted review scenes in new_023/new_088. Thirty-six passed at all three distance limits. Median per-gap errors for lengths 3, 8 and 15 were 0.25, 0.37 and 0.77 pixels; the worst point difference was 6.25 pixels. The withheld tracker coordinates are a reference, not manually localized ground truth, and the acceptance scope does not validate every retained coordinate. Stationary and readily trackable windows can be easier than real edge failures. This is not independent biological validation.
