How priorities and uncertainty are defined

Behavior-review evidence is the union of ambiguous loops, inferred continuation, and disagreement between versions. It is not a probability of error and does not capture every possible uncertainty. Camera bumps cannot be distinguished from animal or tracking motion by these coordinates alone. Missing gaps are not interpolated. Manual flags request review and never approve or exclude a run.
