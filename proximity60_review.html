Movement evidence (optional)

Positive means toward the partner; negative means away. Blank intervals are unknown, often because contact merges the silhouettes. They are not stops. This diagnostic uses the original centered windows; event labels use separate one-sided boundary estimates.

Definitions, uncertainty, and downloads

Entry is center-to-center distance ≤60 original-image pixels; exit is >60 pixels for 0.20 seconds, timestamped at the first outside frame. Short outward excursions are retained within the encounter. Existing PSCs may maintain inferred contact during overlap; they do not prove a measured crossing. Missing tracking without PSC ends observation with an unknown exit.

Initiator means the beetle whose movement contributes to crossing into 60-pixel proximity. Both means both contribute toward the partner; it does not establish who moved first. Leaver means movement contributing to separation. Estimates use separate uninterrupted detections immediately before entry or after exit, at most one second and within 120 pixels. If overlap removes those detections, the answer is unknown. No inferred positions supply individual velocity. First movement onset is a separate, stricter question.

Agreement is required across available 0.20/0.40/0.80-second contexts (short contexts can repeat). These labels describe geometry, not intent or causation. PSC rules and source trajectories remain unchanged; group membership may be indirect in six-beetle videos.
